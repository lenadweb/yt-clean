# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who use YouTube regularly and want to choose what remains visible in
their feed, watch pages, player, and navigation. They encounter the store
screenshots while deciding whether the extension is credible and useful.

## Product Purpose

YouTube Clean is a browser extension that removes unwanted YouTube interface
elements and adds a small set of playback controls. Its promotional imagery
must make the outcome and the breadth of control clear without overstating
what individual settings do.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and unintrusive. The product should feel like a considered
utility that gives viewers control rather than an aggressive ad blocker.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon, glow effects, visual noise, alarmist ad-blocker language,
unnecessary animation, and marketing claims that do not match a real setting.

## Design Principles

1. Show the outcome before describing the controls.
2. Let real product UI establish credibility.
3. Use one red accent deliberately; dark neutrals should stay quiet.
4. Prefer precise, defensible copy to broad promises.
5. Preserve a clear reading order at store-listing size.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast. Static promotional images use the existing red as
an accent, but labels, borders, and layout must also communicate state without
relying solely on color.
